# Tools & Safety

## Tools
- **Hand bender:** for EMT/IMC up to ~1", with angle markings and arrow.
- **Hydraulic bender:** for larger sizes and RMC; use shoe matching conduit size.
- **Conduit roller / large-radius bender:** for segment curves.
- **Level, tape, marker:** measure and verify.
- **Bender shoe & handle extensions:** for leverage and correct radius.

## Safety
- Wear eye protection; metal chips and conduit ends are sharp.
- Support pipe during bend to avoid kinking or spring-back injury.
- Never exceed bender rated capacity; kinked conduit must be replaced.
- De-burr cut ends; ream before bending to prevent injury and wire damage.
- Keep feet clear of bender; use stable footing.
- Verify bends with level before pulling wire.

## Quality checks
- No kinks, wrinkles, or flattening.
- Bend radius ≥ code minimum (typically 4×–10× OD).
- Angles within ±2° of target.
- Conduit remains round; mandrel test if required.
