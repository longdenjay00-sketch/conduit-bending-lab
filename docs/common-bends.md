# Common Bends

## 90° Stub-up
Single bend to turn pipe vertical. Used for boxes, panels, and floor penetrations.

## Offset
Two equal bends to shift pipe around an obstruction while keeping run parallel.

## Back-to-back 90°
Two 90° bends facing opposite directions; used for adjacent conduit runs.

## Saddle
Three bends (side/center/side) to pass over a pipe or obstruction without changing direction.

## Kick
Single small-angle bend to align conduit with a box or fitting.

## Rolling offset
Offset in two planes at once (e.g., around a corner and over an obstruction).

## Segment (multi-bend curve)
Series of small equal bends approximating a large radius curve; used where a single large-radius bender is unavailable.
